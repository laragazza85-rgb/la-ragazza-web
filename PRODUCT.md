# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Primary users are local diners in Villavicencio and visitors discovering where to eat, including Spanish-first residents and English-speaking travelers.
They usually arrive on mobile while deciding where to dine, looking for trust signals, clear menu details, location/contact options, and a fast path to reserve or message.

## Product Purpose
La Ragazza’s website exists to communicate the restaurant’s family identity and culinary personality while making key decisions easy: view the menu, explore atmosphere, and contact the team quickly.
Success means visitors understand what makes the restaurant distinctive, trust the quality, and convert into reservations or direct contact without friction.

## Brand Personality
Warm, artisanal, and family-rooted.
Voice should feel welcoming, honest, and culturally grounded, balancing elegance with approachability rather than formal luxury.

## Anti-references
Avoid overly rustic or cliché “Italian stereotype” aesthetics that feel theatrical instead of authentic.
Avoid generic SaaS-style marketing composition patterns that flatten local identity.
Avoid cold, minimal monochrome presentation that removes warmth and hospitality.

## Design Principles
Lead with hospitality first: every page should feel like an invitation, not a brochure.
Make trust legible quickly: show clear signals of quality, consistency, and real guest experience.
Prioritize decision momentum: menu, contact, and reservation actions should always be easy to find and complete.
Preserve bilingual parity: both locales must feel equally native, complete, and intentional.
Keep craft grounded: use expressive visual details only when they support clarity and brand authenticity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Adopt WCAG 2.2 AA as the baseline standard across content and interactions.
Maintain reduced-motion support and avoid motion-dependent comprehension.
Ensure bilingual content remains readable and navigable on mobile-first usage patterns.
